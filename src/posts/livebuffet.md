*Livebuffet* is a game made by me, my brother, and our dad, for the [Itch.io Mini Jam 178: Monsters](https://itch.io/jam/mini-jam-178-monsters). It's a puzzle game. There's several types of blocks in the game, all of which are alive and awake on some condition. Wake all monsters in a level to complete it. This was our interpretation of the theme, "Everything is alive".

The game was made within the time limit of 72 hours. All assets, including art and music, were made from scratch by this weekend. Art, FX and UI by me, programming and puzzle design by my brother, and music by my dad. Made in Godot with a whole lot of spaghetti. 

We named our trio Dosin Labs, and I'm hoping we'll get to make more games together. This was amazingly fun.
