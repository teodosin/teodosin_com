The cover for **Halysis**' second album: **Unbury the Sun**.

The concept given was quite clear. The album would tell the story of a man trying to remove a great source of passion from his life, thereby metaphorically burying his sun. That passion would haunt him and his dreams from beyond, and in the final act, he would go to dig it up again. 

I started exploring the concept in a few similar ways. I thought the "buried sun" metaphor was elegant, so I wanted to depict it literally. I painted several thumbnails, depicting monuments and gravestones for the sun. Some with a character in the composition, but most without. The band ended up choosing #2 of the sketches, from the more abstract end of the spectrum. A grouping of pillars surrounding an out of sight source of light, implied to be the sun. 

![Unbury the Sun sketches](halysis_unbury/sketches.png)

At the time, I was learning and experimenting with 3D modeling and rendering, and wanted to try making the final cover in 3D and animate it in some sort of loop. Doing this revealed a miscommunication I had with the band: I had assumed that what they liked in their chosen sketch was the subject matter, but what they were more fond of was the style. I took responsibility for the misunderstanding and offered to start over, painting the cover properly rather than trying to model and animate it. 

![Unbury the Sun, Work in Progress](halysis_unbury/wip.png)

The new (or old) direction was immediately more pleasing to both them and me. For the future, this project taught me not to be trying new things on the client's expense. Stick to what I'm good at, and expand my skillset on my own time.
