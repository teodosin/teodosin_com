In 2021 I cofounded an event startup, Skene, with three other people. The company was intended to be a platform for events, providing event discovery, ticketing, and other services for both organisers and attendees. My main role was web design and frontend development. 

After working on the site for the year, we decided to shut down the business. We simply didn't gain enough momentum to stay motivated, young and inexperienced as we were. We focused too much on polish and not enough on validation and user acquisition. Of course we learned a lot, and it was valuable to get a taste of what it's like to run a business so early in our careers.
