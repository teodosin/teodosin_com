Heart of the World is a series of graphite drawings I made in 2017-2018. It was my first serious attempt at completing a cohesive series, and one I'm still very fond of. Inspiration was taken from various sources, but the imagery mainly came from a few dreams I had at that time. The drawings are surreal metaphors of aspects of the human condition, and therefore world. 

Each drawing is accompanied by a short text, which I suppose could be considered a poem. Meant to be subtle in their meaning, which perhaps they are not, in hindsight. 

The series got a spiritual successor in the form of [Name of the World](name-of-the-world), a drawing I finished in late 2024 and my first completed fine art drawing in many years. All seven of the drawings were exhibited in my first solo exhibition in January of 2025, also titled Heart of the World. 

![Regret of the World](heartoftheworld/1st.jpg)

*"By that time they've shed a hundred faces already. Enough for a life, they may think. They may decline, then resist, then forget. This one they've had forever. It fits, it comforts, it's them. When they suffocate, they will remember nothing, and regret everything."*

- Regret of the World

![Hubris of the World](heartoftheworld/2nd.jpg)

*"Pride in the waters, watching the horizon on all sides. Nothing in sight could bring it down. All avoid the flutter of its wings. It paid no mind that in every moment of rest, under the surface, the currents eroded them."*

- Hubris of the World

![Denial of the World](heartoftheworld/3rd.png)

*"So far away and harmless, not a concern. Looming in the distance, but not urgent. Its burn and glow, not a threat. Each glance an invitation to get closer. Soon its presence will be felt. No glances after that."*

- Denial of the World

![Patience of the World](heartoftheworld/4th.jpg)

*"One day, he believed, this long wait would be rewarded. He would remain still, and the world would come to him. One day. That one day will come; he'll be brought together with the world. Neither will outlive the other."*

- Patience of the World

![Avarice of the World](heartoftheworld/5th.jpg)

*"It follows its fixation, to seize a piece of existence. Obsession takes it to where it won't belong. It scrambles in the depths for what it wants to want. Finally near, it'll see that the world will not allow it."*

- Avarice of the World

![Enmity of the World](heartoftheworld/6th.jpg)

*"They hear it in everything. The beat of a heart, keeping them alive. Always there announcing its presence. A pulse that would not cease. They turned the world over in search for it. In time, they found it. Being found killed it."*

- Enmity of the World
