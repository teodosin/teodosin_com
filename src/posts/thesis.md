My Master's Thesis for my degree in Visual Communication Design. In it, I flesh out a lot of my thoughts on creative tools and complexity that I had been developing during my master's studies, and present my initial concept for **[Karta](karta)**.

You can download the whole thesis **[from here](https://aaltodoc.aalto.fi/bitstreams/ec1d323f-1126-4a38-a795-7da5e4ac03e3/download)**. 

For a summary, here is the abstract.

```
For any creative working on a project, it is vital for them to be able to pick out and see how the different elements interact with each other to form the experience of the creative work. As works grow in complexity, creating and maintaining overviews of them becomes increasingly difficult and cumbersome. Crucially, the substance of the work, the project files and assets, become disconnected from the conceptual structure of the work.

This thesis explores the use of dynamic node graphs as a tool for managing complexity in creative projects and organising a creative's entire body of work. It proposes a novel approach to file system navigation and project visualisation, drawing on theories of spacial cognition and conceptual mapping. The research examines node graphs as the most appropriate visual form for abstract thinking and presents a software concept and prototype, Karta, as a concrete implementation for the theoretical concepts.

Karta leverages node graph principles to create a unified, navigable network of a creative's digital material, allowing for intuitive exploration and connection-making across projects. The thesis argues that by more closely aligning external representations with internal cognitive models, creatives can more effectively manage complex projects, maintain consistency, and potentially uncover new insights in their work. While acknowledging the challenges of implementing such a system, the research suggests that this approach could significantly enhance creative workflows and project management in various artistic and design fields.
```
