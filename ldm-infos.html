Legal Design Infographics

August 2021

I interned for a legal design company in the summer of 2021. While there, I was tasked with creating infographics for a book the founder was writing about legal design. It was the last project I did while there.

They ended up going with a different direction for the infographics. These clearly needed refinements, and I wasn’t there anymore to do them. I’m still happy with the work I did though. With some adjustments, I think this cohesive style of vector graphics could be a good fit for an educational book on some topic. This was my first internship and therefore job in my field(s), and though there wasn’t a senior designer there to guide me, I learned a lot from the experience.
